About me

Hello - I'm Ryan. I've been programming for many years, and I've spent the last five of them putting that experience to work in research, completing a PhD in machine learning and natural language processing. My work focuses on classification, uncertainty analysis and information theory applied to real-world language data.

Alongside research, I've taught core computing concepts internationally over the past few years - in Guangzhou, China, and on campuses across Hong Kong - working with students of a wide range of backgrounds and abilities. Whether you're taking your first steps in code, preparing for exams or coursework, or getting to grips with machine learning, I'll meet you at your level and build from there.
